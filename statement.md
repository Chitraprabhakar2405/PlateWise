# PlateWise - Project Statement

## Problem Statement

College messes often prepare food based on estimated attendance, which can lead to over-preparation and unnecessary food waste. At the same time, preparing too little food can result in insufficient meals for students.

PlateWise is designed to help manage meal attendance, record food preparation and consumption, analyze food waste, and provide preparation quantity recommendations based on historical consumption data.

## Scope of the Project

The scope of PlateWise includes:

- Recording student meal attendance
- Managing meal and food item information
- Recording quantities of food prepared and consumed
- Calculating food waste and waste percentage
- Classifying waste as Low, Moderate, or High
- Generating waste alerts
- Maintaining meal records using file-based storage
- Providing preparation quantity recommendations using historical consumption

## Target Users

- College mess managers
- Mess staff
- Students for meal attendance recording

## High-Level Features

- Student and mess manager management
- Meal and food item management
- Meal attendance recording
- Food waste calculation
- Waste percentage analysis
- Waste classification and alerts
- Waste history and analytics
- Preparation quantity recommendation
- File-based storage of meal records
